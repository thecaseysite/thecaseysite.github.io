About Casey Simpson

Casey West Simpson (born April 6, 2004) is an American actor. He is best known for his roles as Ricky Harper on the Nickelodeon sitcom Nicky, Ricky, Dicky & Dawn, and Kyle Keeley in Escape From Mr. Lemoncello's Library.
